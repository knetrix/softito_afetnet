Account and Data Deletion

Effective / last updated: 12 November 2026

AfetNet is a non-commercial training project built in the SoftITO Mobile Development programme (Term 4). It is not an official service; in an emergency call 112.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open the Profile tab in the AfetNet app.
  2. Tap Delete Account at the bottom and confirm.
  3. Enter your password again for security.

Once the password is verified, deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

2. What is deleted?

  • Your profile: full name, email, phone, address, emergency contact and health information
  • The name and phone stored with your help requests
  • Your panic/safe signal
  • Your sign-in account (email and password)

3. What is kept?

The type, location, number of people and status of help requests remain as operational records with no personal information, to keep disaster coordination records complete. These records are kept for the duration of the training project and deleted when it ends.

4. If you cannot access the app

Email mustafa.erso845@gmail.com from the email address of your account to request deletion. Requests are handled within 30 days at the latest.
