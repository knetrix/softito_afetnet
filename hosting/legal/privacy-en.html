LEGAL DOCUMENT

Privacy Policy and Personal Data Notice

Effective / updated: 12 November 2026

AfetNet is a non-commercial training project built in the SoftITO Mobile Development programme (Term 4). It is not an official service; in an emergency call 112.

1. Data controller and contact

Data controller for AfetNet: SoftITO Mobile Development Term 4 — Group 2 project team (AfetNet training project). Contact: mustafa.erso845@gmail.com. Requests are received by email.

2. Data that may be processed

  • Account and profile: name, email address, phone number and user role
  • Optional profile details: emergency contact, address, physical details and medical information that may require explicit consent
  • Disaster coordination: help requests, panic/safe signals, submitted descriptions, incidents and team assignment status
  • Location: device location with permission and location or address provided by the user
  • Technical and communication data: notification device tokens, error and security records (where enabled)

3. Purposes

Account creation and authentication; routing help requests to authorized responders; coordinating teams and supplies; delivering area-based alerts; protecting the service; and handling user requests.

4. Collection and legal grounds

Data is collected electronically through app forms, device permissions (location, notifications) and use of the service. Legal grounds (KVKK Art. 5/2): account, identity and contact data because they are necessary to provide the service (para. c); panic signals, help requests and location because they are necessary to protect the life or physical integrity of a person unable to give consent (para. b); service security and coordinator verification on the basis of legitimate interest (para. f). Medical information is special-category data and is processed only on the separate explicit consent you give in Profile (KVKK Art. 6); without consent these fields stay empty. Reading this notice does not itself constitute consent.

5. Recipients and transfers

Name, phone, address and medical information are visible only to verified field coordinators; other users cannot see them. Data is not shared with third parties for sale or advertising. Data is stored on Google Firebase (Google LLC) infrastructure in its Europe region (eur3), i.e. outside Türkiye. This transfer is made only to operate the service and in line with Article 9 of the KVKK.

6. Location and notifications

Location permissions can be managed in device settings. Disabling them may limit map features or nearby alerts. Area-based notifications cannot guarantee receipt; delivery depends on connectivity, permissions and device conditions.

7. Retention and deletion

Your personal data is kept while your account exists and is deleted immediately with Delete Account. Operational records with no personal information are kept for the duration of the training project and deleted when it ends. No separate backups are taken. Deletion steps are on the Account Deletion page.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 you may learn whether your data is processed, request information, learn purposes and recipients, seek correction or deletion, and exercise your other legal rights by writing to mustafa.erso845@gmail.com from the email address of your account. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

9. Changes

This notice is updated when the app's processing activities change; the current version is always on this page.
