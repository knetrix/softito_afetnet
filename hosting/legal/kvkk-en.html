KVKK Privacy Notice

Effective / last updated: 12 November 2026

AfetNet is a non-commercial training project built in the SoftITO Mobile Development programme (Term 4). It is not an official service; in an emergency call 112.

1. Data controller

Data controller: SoftITO Mobile Development Term 4 — Group 2 project team (AfetNet training project). Contact: mustafa.erso845@gmail.com. Requests are received by email.

This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

2. Personal data processed

  • Identity and contact: full name, email, phone number
  • Location: the device location when you send a panic signal or help request; optionally a saved address
  • Emergency contact: name, relationship and phone (optional)
  • Health data (special category): blood type, medications, chronic conditions/allergies, birth year and weight — only with separate explicit consent
  • Help records: help request type, number of people, address hint, description, status and assigned team; panic/safe signal

3. Purposes

Delivering help requests and panic signals to verified field coordinators during a disaster, dispatching teams, merging requests from the same spot, sending regional evacuation alerts and managing your account.

4. Who can access it?

Name, phone, address and health data are visible only to you and to field coordinators whose accounts have been verified. Other users cannot see them. Data is stored on Google Firebase (Google LLC) infrastructure in its Europe region (eur3), i.e. outside Türkiye. This transfer is made only to operate the service and in line with Article 9 of the KVKK.

5. Collection method and legal basis

Data is collected electronically through the forms in the mobile app and device permissions (location, notifications). Health data is processed only on the basis of your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 6); without consent these fields stay empty. Legal grounds (KVKK Art. 5/2): account, identity and contact data because they are necessary to provide the service (para. c); panic signals, help requests and location because they are necessary to protect the life or physical integrity of a person unable to give consent (para. b); service security and coordinator verification on the basis of legitimate interest (para. f).

6. Your rights

For your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK (access, rectification, erasure, objection, etc.) contact the address above. You can also delete your account and personal information yourself in the app under Profile → Delete Account; see Account Deletion.
